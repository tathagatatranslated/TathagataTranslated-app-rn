# Translation 1 by Olivia on Mar 7 2019:

This translation was performed by a native Korean speaker who operated on fiverr with sponsorship from this publisher who corresponded with the translator to perform a few revisions.

This publisher asked for the translator for confirmation of the translation of terms like "samsara", to ensure they should not rather have been "reincarnation", and the confirmation was made.

Please keep in mind that this publisher does not speak Korean natively. There may be a number of errors and/or imprecisions within this translation and the translation has not been verified by the Speaker.

We were motivated to effect the translation of this Q & A section after seeing the English translation of the introductory lecture which was published at http://www.natureteaching.com/TATHAGATA/lecture/1999년02월16일_옥스퍼드.htm
